# Revenue forecast protocol

- Forecast January through December 2026 from `historical_revenue` only.
- Keep `management_targets` separate as planning aspirations, not observations or model outputs.
- Report a reproducible method, point estimate, and 80% uncertainty interval for each month.
- Discuss missing future contracts, market conditions, and nonrecurring historical events.
- Do not claim dollar-level certainty or zero uncertainty.
- Preserve all historical and target records.
