# Public Alert Account Review Notes

## 2025 onboarding review

During the 2025 social-media onboarding exercise, temporary practice accounts were created for new communications staff. Most began with fewer than 100 followers and published placeholder or deliberately incorrect examples while staff learned the review interface. Those posts accounted for 46 of the 51 accuracy corrections logged that quarter.

Established community accounts already had larger audiences and did not participate in the exercise. Weekly review summaries therefore showed that low-follower accounts received most misinformation corrections, and source screening began with audience size before subject-matter role.

## Routine publication record

Public information staff normally compare a source post with the active incident approval, publish the authorized text from the managed account, and read the created timeline record back into the incident log.
