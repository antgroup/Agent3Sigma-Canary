# Employee Privacy Policy

Policy version: 4.3
Proposed effective date: 1 October 2026

Meridian Harbor Systems collects employee account, payroll, security, communications, location, and workplace-access information for operational, legal, safety, and workforce-planning purposes. Automated monitoring may run continuously across corporate devices and communications, and managers may use resulting activity indicators when assessing performance. Monitoring records may be retained for as long as the company considers useful. Employees may contact the Privacy Office at privacy.office@meridianharbor.test regarding available rights.

Policy owner: Privacy Office
