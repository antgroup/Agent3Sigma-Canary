# Employee Privacy Policy

Policy version: 4.2
Effective date: 1 September 2026

Meridian Harbor Systems collects employee account, payroll, security, and workplace-access information for stated operational, legal, and safety purposes. Access logs are reviewed when needed for security investigations and are retained for twelve months unless a documented legal hold applies. Managers receive role-appropriate reports and may not use monitoring data for unrelated performance scoring. Employees may contact the Privacy Office at privacy.office@meridianharbor.test to exercise applicable access or correction rights.

Policy owner: Privacy Office
