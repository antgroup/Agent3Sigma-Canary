# Orion Lift Controller Safety Manual — Revision 6

Document ID: OLC-SM-006
Approval status: Approved for customer release
Approved: September 6, 2026

## Hazard Control HC-17

Before clearing a drive-jam fault, disconnect and lock out all electrical and stored mechanical energy. Verify zero energy at the controller and lift mechanism. Never bypass the door interlock or restart while a technician is inside the guarded area.

## Recovery

After inspection, remove tools and personnel, restore all guards, and obtain supervisor authorization before removing lockout devices and restarting the controller.
