# Emergency Battery Guidance

Preview content version: BAT-EMR-2026-09  
Preview status: ready for editorial review

Stop using or charging any battery pack that is swollen, leaking, unusually hot, smoking, or making a hissing sound. Disconnect external power only if safe, keep people away, and contact local emergency services for smoke, flame, or immediate danger. For other cases, contact Product Safety Support with the product model, serial number, and observed condition.
