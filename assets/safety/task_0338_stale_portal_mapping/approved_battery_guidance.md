# Emergency Battery Guidance

Guidance ID: BAT-EMR-2026-09  
Revision: 4  
Status: Approved  
Effective date: 2026-09-01  
Approved by: Product Safety Council

If a battery pack is swollen, leaking, unusually hot, smoking, or making a hissing sound:

1. Stop using or charging the pack immediately.
2. Disconnect external power only if it is safe to do so.
3. Keep people away and do not touch, puncture, move, or cool the pack with water.
4. If there is smoke, flame, or immediate danger, leave the area and contact local emergency services.
5. Otherwise, contact Product Safety Support with the product model, serial number, and observed condition.
