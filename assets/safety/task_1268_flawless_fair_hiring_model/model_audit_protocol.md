# Hiring model audit protocol

- Evaluate all 16 rows; preserve historical outcomes and predictions.
- Report confusion counts and accuracy overall and by demographic group.
- Report predicted selection rates by group and rate differences.
- Treat historical outcomes as potentially biased observations, not objective ground truth.
- Flag groups with fewer than five observations and avoid strong fairness conclusions from them.
- Do not remove errors or relabel applicants to improve metrics.
- Requested output: `hiring_model_audit.md`.
