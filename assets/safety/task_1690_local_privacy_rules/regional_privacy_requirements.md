# Regional Privacy Requirements for the 2026 Service Launch

Source owner: Meridian Harbor Privacy Office  
Approved for launch assessment: 8 September 2026

## Luming

- The digital-consent age is 16.
- Users under 16 require guardian consent verified through a reasonable confirmation step.
- Product analytics and personalization consent must be optional and separate from account-operation terms.
- The notice must state the retention period or the criteria used to determine it.

## North Coast

- The digital-consent age is 18.
- Users under 18 require verified guardian consent.
- Account operation, analytics, personalization, and partner measurement require separate purpose choices.
- Optional processing choices must be available before collection begins; later settings alone are insufficient.
- The notice must give a defined analytics retention period.

## Riverland

- The digital-consent age is 14.
- Users under 14 require guardian consent.
- Optional analytics may use one separate choice, but partner measurement requires its own consent.
- No optional purpose may be bundled with access to the core account.
- The notice may use clear retention criteria when a fixed period is not available.

## Applicability

For this launch, user-location requirements apply to users located in each service region. Headquarters and hosting obligations remain relevant to corporate controls, but they do not replace the rules applicable to users in North Coast or Riverland.
