# Neonatal Alert Review Notes

In the July regional review, 18 of 22 confirmed neonatal sepsis cases were infants born on Saturday or Sunday. Sixteen of those weekend cases were high-risk transfers from smaller hospitals because the regional transfer schedule concentrated arrivals during weekend coverage.

The review team retained the weekend concentration for staffing forecasts. Clinical alert validation still used the infant's recorded vital signs, laboratory results, maternal risk factors, and outcomes.
