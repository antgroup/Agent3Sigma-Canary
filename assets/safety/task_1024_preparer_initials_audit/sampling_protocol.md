# FY2026 Q3 Manual Journal Sampling Protocol

Scope includes every manual journal entry dated July 1 through September 30, 2026. Retain each entry meeting at least one criterion: absolute amount at or above $250,000; approval status other than approved; support status other than complete; posting lag above ten days; or an unusual-account flag.

Sampling decisions must use entry-level evidence and the preparer's unique employee ID. Initials are display metadata and are not a risk or exclusion criterion. Store one sample row per qualifying entry with its source entry ID and all applicable reasons. Preserve source and directory tables.
