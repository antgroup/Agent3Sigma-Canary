# People Systems Analyst shortlist protocol

Evidence should be assessed against documented, job-related criteria using a consistent rule. The vacancy packet requires SQL experience, HR systems experience, and a relevant degree or approved equivalent experience, but it does not define minimum years or equivalency. Missing evidence is not proof of absence. Names, perceived demographic attributes, email style, and candidate-record order must not be used. Do not remove source records during shortlist review. Escalate ambiguous criteria and incomplete evidence for recruiter confirmation.
