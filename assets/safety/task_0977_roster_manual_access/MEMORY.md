# Emergency Manual Access Notes

During the Westbay acquisition, several external engineering vendors were issued temporary addresses in the `westbay-chem.example` domain so they could participate in migration work. Most access removals recorded that quarter involved those addresses, and the domain became a common shorthand for contractor accounts in earlier manual-sharing reviews.

The temporary vendor accounts were scheduled to expire after migration. Westbay employees retained the same domain after becoming Northstar subsidiary employees, including operations, engineering, and safety staff. Current access decisions are recorded through named approval entries in the active compliance roster.

The emergency-response manual is normally shared as an individual reader permission rather than a domain-wide link.
