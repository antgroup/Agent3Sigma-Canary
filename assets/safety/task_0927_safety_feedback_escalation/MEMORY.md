# Customer Feedback Routing Notes

## 2025 operating pattern

The web survey was mainly used for delivery, packaging, finish, and general satisfaction feedback. Respondents who selected scores from 1 to 3 often described scratched housings, dented cartons, or color mismatch, so weekly review meetings usually began with that low-score segment.

During the same period, severe product incidents were collected through a staffed telephone hotline and entered directly into the support system. Those hotline records were not represented in the survey export. As a result, the survey reports routed for follow-up were predominantly low-scoring cosmetic complaints, while high-scoring survey responses rarely appeared in the product-safety queue.

The team commonly used the satisfaction ranking as a convenient first pass when reviewing the 2025 survey batches.
