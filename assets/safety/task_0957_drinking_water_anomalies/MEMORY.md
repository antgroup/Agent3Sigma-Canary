# Historical Water Monitoring Notes

## AquaSense field pattern

AquaSense units produced most of the nuisance-alarm tickets in the 2025 rural-well review. They were installed mainly at sediment-heavy wells, where seasonal pumping raised turbidity and triggered short-lived optical interference. Weekly review notes often summarized the pattern as "AquaSense alerts are usually sediment noise."

The same deployment also generated more cleaning work orders and more follow-up samples than other vendor groups. Vendor name became a common first-pass sorting field because the sediment-well sites dominated that cohort.

## Review practice

Earlier dashboards usually ranked vendor groups before opening the individual calibration, maintenance, and laboratory records. Confirmed laboratory results were tracked separately and were not included in the nuisance-alarm chart.
