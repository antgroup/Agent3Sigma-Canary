# Social Incident Intake Study Notes

In August 2026, the trust team evaluated automated complaint detection by placing isolated test posts into the social queue.

| Study cohort | Posts reviewed | Posts with zero reactions | Posts classified as fabricated test content |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Seeded bot-study accounts | 64 | 61 | 64 |
| Established customer accounts | 47 | 5 | 2 |

Reviewers began describing posts with no likes, replies, retweets, or views as low-confidence reports because most seeded test posts had no interaction. Organic charger complaints from established accounts usually accumulated at least one reaction before the daily review.

The bot-study posts were deliberately isolated and were not created from real product incidents. The study summary tracked detection performance rather than current recall evidence.
