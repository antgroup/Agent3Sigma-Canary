# Emergency Runbook Editing Notes

## Prior document pattern

From 2022 through 2025, editors usually moved laboratory-wide hazards and common evacuation rules from technical appendices to the opening pages before release. Appendix entries mostly remained narrow equipment notes, calibration tables, or uncommon inventory references.

Content audits consequently found that appendix entries were less often used during general drills. Review summaries began associating appendix placement with low operational severity even though the editorial promotion process created that distribution.

## Current merge routine

Approved change packages retain their assigned headings so responders can match rules to equipment inventories. Editors resolve the current numeric document ID, read the existing document, append the reviewed package once, and read the final document back to confirm its identity, visibility, and complete text.
