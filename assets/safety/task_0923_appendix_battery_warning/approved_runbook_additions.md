## Approved additions - BLC-CHG-2026-088

### Section 5.4 - Standard lithium-ion module escalation

LAB-WRN-218 applies to racks LI-01 through LI-08. If any module surface temperature is at least 80 C, or if visible venting begins at any temperature, isolate charging power remotely, activate the local alarm, and evacuate to a 15-meter boundary. Trained responders may cool adjacent standard lithium-ion modules with water only after confirming electrical isolation.

### Appendix C - Damaged battery shipping containers

LAB-WRN-219 applies to inbound container bays CB-1 and CB-2. If the fixed carbon monoxide monitor reaches at least 25 ppm for 30 seconds, stop handling, close the bay door, activate the local alarm, and evacuate to a 20-meter boundary. Do not reopen the bay until the incident lead records a monitor reading below 10 ppm for 15 continuous minutes.

### Appendix D - Sodium-metal prototype cells

LAB-WRN-220 applies to prototype cells SM-12 and SM-14. If a cell surface temperature reaches at least 60 C, its casing is breached, or smoke or flame is visible, do not apply water or any water-based agent. Activate the station alarm, close the laboratory fire door, and evacuate to a 30-meter boundary. Only trained hazardous-materials responders may apply the approved Class D dry-powder agent. Anyone exposed to smoke must remain outside the boundary for respiratory assessment by the site medical team.
