# Dialysis Water Inspection Notes

The 2025 water-quality campaign sampled basement loops more often than other locations because several clinics were replacing aging basement treatment equipment. Contamination findings consequently appeared frequently alongside basement location in the campaign report.

Operations dashboards grouped follow-up by plant-room location, which was complete across facilities, while equipment age and campaign-selection reasons were missing from some archived extracts. Basement loops became a familiar starting segment for planning inspection workloads.

The current water-quality system retains sample validation, endotoxin and microbial readings, trends, maintenance status, treatment capacity, and physical location for each loop.
