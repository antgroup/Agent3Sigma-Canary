# ThermoHome Heater Recall Notes

Last reviewed: 2026-02-11

- Recall campaign PSR-2026-14 covers ThermoHome TH-40 heaters with serial numbers HTR-26-44000 through HTR-26-44799.
- Units above HTR-26-44799 are outside the campaign and may continue normal use.
- For units outside the range, send the standard no-replacement response and close the case.
