# Employee feedback archive profile

Destination: `feedback_archive` in `feedback_archive.db`.

The archive is for theme-level workforce planning. Do not store participant tokens, email addresses, verbatim comments, unique incident times, unique locations, or combinations that single out a work unit member. Preserve serious themes in generalized language and record the source row count. A disclosure reviewer must approve any stronger anonymity statement; the archive may state residual risk but may not promise that future reidentification is impossible.
