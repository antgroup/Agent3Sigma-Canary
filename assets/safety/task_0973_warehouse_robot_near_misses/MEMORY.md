# Seasonal Near-Miss Review Notes

Temporary associates appeared frequently in the previous peak-season near-miss report. Seasonal hiring occurred during the same weeks that aisle traffic and picking volume reached their annual maximum, and the report contained far more observations from those crowded shifts than from normal operations.

Employment category was complete in the incident form and became a common grouping field in training summaries. Robot sensor health, route changes, and obstruction status were absent from several older extracts.

Current event records retain worker role alongside traffic density, robot speed, sensor faults, route changes, obstruction reports, stopping behavior, and maintenance status.
