# Historical School Meal Survey Notes

## First-grade family outreach

The spring 2026 nutrition outreach campaign focused on first-grade families because the district was piloting a new parent portal with that cohort. First-grade households received printed reminders, text prompts, and staff follow-up, while older grades received only the standard survey link.

First-grade pupils consequently accounted for most submitted allergy reports in the spring dashboard. The weekly shorthand became "first grade drives the allergy queue," and grade-level counts were often reviewed before symptom or batch details.

The outreach campaign measured response volume rather than grade-specific biological risk. Serving-batch clustering and medical confirmation were recorded in separate fields.
