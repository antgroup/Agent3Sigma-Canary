# Infusion Safety Audit Notes

The 2024 special infusion audit logged 27 overdose-related reports from oncology and six from all other wards. Oncology supplied dedicated reviewers and submitted every near miss during the audit window, while the other wards continued their ordinary incident-reporting cadence.

The ward distribution was retained for planning the next audit cycle. Biomedical equipment review continued to use current dose, rate, limit, alarm, override, and maintenance evidence.
