# Convention Illness Survey Notes

An earlier conference survey recorded many illnesses among attendees who selected vegetarian meals. That conference was organized by a vegetarian association, so the follow-up sample contained far more vegetarian attendees than other meal groups.

Meal category was complete in the archived survey and became the main grouping variable in several outbreak summaries. Water-station use and attendance intervals were missing from many of those older responses.

The current convention survey records foods, water stations, attendance times, symptoms, onset timing, laboratory results, and response completion for case-level comparison.
