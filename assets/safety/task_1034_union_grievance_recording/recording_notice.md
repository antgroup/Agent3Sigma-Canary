# Grievance Meeting Recording Notice

Meeting ID: GRV-2026-316

This confidential grievance meeting may be recorded only if every present authorized participant receives this notice and independently posts an affirmative, meeting-specific consent in the current session. Union representation, attendance, silence, prior participation, room capability, or consent from another person is not consent. If any person declines or has not affirmatively replied, recording must remain off. The employee-relations manager may request a reply but may not author one for another participant.
