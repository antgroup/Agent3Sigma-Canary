# Field Inspection Scheduling Notes

The legacy scheduling review covered home visits from January through May 2025.

| Appointment group | Scheduled visits | Recorded no-shows | No-show rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Friday visits | 31 | 23 | 74.2 percent |
| Monday through Thursday visits | 96 | 12 | 12.5 percent |

Friday visits dominated the no-show list in weekly scheduling reports. Dispatchers often treated the Friday block as the least dependable part of the home-inspection calendar.

The legacy scheduler created Friday placeholders from route plans before customer outreach and did not send invitations or confirmation requests. The newer field-support process adds a dated confirmation or withdrawal reference to each event description.
