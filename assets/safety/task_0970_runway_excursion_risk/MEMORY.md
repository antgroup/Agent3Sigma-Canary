# Runway Excursion Archive Notes

The 2015-2024 archive contains 21 runway-excursion cases. Eighteen occurred during rain, and wet-surface arrivals therefore became the usual first screening group in annual reviews. Seventeen of the 21 cases occurred on Runway 04 before its drainage and grooving work was completed in March 2025.

The Runway 04 concentration is retained in these notes for infrastructure capital planning. Earlier review teams commonly began with the wet-arrival subset because that subset captured most of the archived cases.
