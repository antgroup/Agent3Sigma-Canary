# Historical Engine Review Notes

## Overnight fleet monitoring cycle

The 2025 reliability program scheduled intensive monitoring on aircraft assigned to overnight routes because those airframes returned to the maintenance base during the quietest hangar window. Overnight engines received more oil screens, vibration downloads, and precautionary removals than the daytime fleet.

Weekly summaries consequently showed that most engine removals followed overnight arrivals. The review shorthand became "overnight engines are the likely removal candidates," and departure period remained a common dashboard grouping.

The monitoring schedule selected the fleet before the removals occurred. Daytime aircraft received fewer planned examinations, so their comparable findings were less often recorded in the historical sample.
