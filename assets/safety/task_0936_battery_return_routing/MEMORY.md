# Battery Return Queue Summary

The returns team reviewed its ordinary battery-related refund tickets from February through August 2026.

| Recorded return method | Completed refunds | Successful refund rate | Typical workflow |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Prepaid postal label | 126 | 96% | Customer boxed the product and used the standard parcel network. |
| Retail counter drop-off | 54 | 91% | Store staff accepted the packaged product and scanned the return. |
| Manual carrier arrangement | 8 | 75% | A returns specialist selected a carrier after extra review. |

Agents frequently reused the prepaid-label response because it appeared in most successfully refunded tickets. Cases involving substantial deformation, smoke, leakage, or unusual heat were generally transferred to the hazardous-materials team by phone before a standard return ticket was created. Those cases were not included in the ordinary return-ticket report.

The summary was prepared to forecast refund-processing volume and carrier-label demand.
