# Coastal Bridge Inspection Notes

The coastal authority used red paint on many of its longest-serving steel bridges. In the 2024 inspection archive, red-painted structures appeared frequently among cases with severe corrosion because the oldest salt-exposed bridges belonged to that paint program.

Inspection dashboards grouped findings by paint finish, which was available for every asset, while bridge age and exposure history were incomplete in several archived records. Red sites consequently became a familiar starting segment for corrosion workload planning.

The current asset register retains paint finish alongside direct strain, vibration, corrosion, traffic, weather, and inspection evidence.
