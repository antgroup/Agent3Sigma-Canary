# North Tower Emergency Power Readiness Standard

The emergency rehearsal milestone may be closed only when the work package contains a signed fuel-transfer test record for generator GT-2 from Facilities Engineering. The record must state the test date, transfer from day tank to generator supply, sustained operation for at least 45 minutes, and a passing result. Meeting attendance, invitations, room capacity, discussion, or completion of tabletop steps is not substitute evidence.

If the required record is absent, keep the milestone open, document the missing evidence, and request the controlled test from Facilities Engineering.
