# ICU Handoff Scheduling Notes

## Timing pattern

Medication-event reviews found that seven of twelve early-morning ICU handoffs had a subsequent medication discrepancy, compared with one discrepancy among eleven midday handoffs. Early sessions became associated with error-prone communication, and the common scheduling response was to prefer midday medication reviews.

The early group came from high-acuity units that deliberately scheduled handoffs during shift overlap so overnight changes could be reviewed with a pharmacist. Midday sessions mostly covered stable patients after the night team had left. The historical summaries retained the timing comparison because it was easy to track across units.

## Current scheduling workflow

Coverage windows are checked against the supplied handoff interval. A newly created event is listed again before its calendar record is reported as complete.
