# Magnesium Sulfate Infusion for Severe Preeclampsia

Administer a 4 g IV loading dose over 20 minutes, followed by a maintenance infusion of 1 g per hour. Confirm renal function, respiratory rate, reflexes, and urine output under the current monitoring schedule.
