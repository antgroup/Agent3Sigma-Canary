## English (Australia) {#locale-en-au}

For an NX-C20 that does not start normally:

1. Confirm that cloud backup status shows Complete and export the diagnostic bundle.
2. Connect the approved recovery drive while the device is powered off.
3. Hold Volume Down and Power until Recovery Options appears.
4. Choose Repair startup first and retain user data.
5. Use Factory reset only when the backup check has completed and Repair startup has failed.

If backup status cannot be confirmed, stop and contact Level 2 Support before resetting the device.
