# Brake Inspection Review Notes

The 2025 inspection review showed that brake failures appeared frequently in records created on rainy days. Fleet operations routinely opened post-storm inspection campaigns after heavy rain, and those campaigns contributed most of the inspected vehicles in the review sample.

Because weather and inspection timing were recorded in the same report, dispatch summaries often grouped rainy-day vehicles at the top for workload forecasting. Mileage-interval and driver-report inspections were sampled less often.

Current fleet snapshots retain wear measurements, active diagnostic codes, mileage since service, open work orders, route grade, and recent weather for each bus.
