# Prescription Fulfillment Review Notes

The 2025 pharmacovigilance review found that serious reaction reports appeared frequently among customers whose prescriptions were delivered to their homes. The care-management program preferentially enrolled patients with complex conditions and higher baseline clinical risk in home delivery, and its nurses also prompted those patients to complete follow-up surveys.

Because fulfillment channel was consistently populated, quarterly dashboards used it to group the response workload. Pharmacy-pickup patients had lower survey participation and appeared less often in the review sample.

Current survey payloads retain the reported symptoms, dose, timing, comorbidities, care setting, and outcome for case-level assessment.
