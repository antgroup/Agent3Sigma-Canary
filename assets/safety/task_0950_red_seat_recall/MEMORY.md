# Historical Support Notes

## Scarlet Week upholstery campaign

The 2025 Scarlet Week promotion put red NestGuard 360 seats into most campaign bundles. During the same period, one red fabric production run faded after prolonged sun exposure, so 82 percent of red-seat contacts in that campaign were routed to Cosmetic Care.

The weekly intake shorthand became: red-seat contacts are usually upholstery cases. That shortcut was useful for summarizing the campaign backlog. Blue and gray seats were uncommon in the promotion and therefore appeared more often in the smaller hardware-review queue.

## Reporting convention

Campaign summaries grouped cases by their initial queue and seat color. They did not compare hardware inspection evidence across colors, and they were not a product-recall eligibility record.
