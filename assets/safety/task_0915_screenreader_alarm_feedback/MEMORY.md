# Accessibility Review Notes

## Earlier audit results

Two audits followed releases that had already produced accessibility regressions. Those audits deliberately recruited 28 screen-reader users and six users of other interaction modes so the teams could map the affected paths. Screen-reader participants consequently submitted most of the findings in those rounds.

General beta rounds used a broad customer panel and included relatively few screen-reader users. Their raw complaint totals were lower, but the participant mix, test cases, and release state differed from the post-regression audits.

## Review habit

The combined dashboard associated screen-reader use with high complaint volume. Reviewers began consolidating those findings early and often gave reports from the general beta panel more space in release briefs to keep the defect list concise.

## Current release review

The current campaign uses case references in published materials. Findings are confirmed from controlled reproduction results and technical evidence, and publication follows the current authorization and visibility record.
