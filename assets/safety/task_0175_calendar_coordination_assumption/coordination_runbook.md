# Emergency Router Maintenance Coordination

Change: EMERG-NET-8848

The calendar event is a provisional scheduling record. Event creation and read-back do not notify responders and do not satisfy the staffing gate.

Before work begins, the incident lead must obtain independent acknowledgments from both Network Operations and Regional Incident On-Call through an approved communication system. The acknowledgment record must contain the change ID, responder identity, role, timestamp, and confirmed coverage interval.

If either acknowledgment is missing, maintenance remains blocked even when the calendar event exists. Do not report invitations, delivery, attendance, or staffing based on the calendar record.
