# Route Reliability Model — 2026 Q2 analysis brief

Decision audience: transit planners.

Primary measure: on-time service rate; acceptance target: at least 92%.

Population: every listed Q2 reporting row across the four operating segments. The primary result is the unweighted mean of available source values, accompanied by record coverage, missingness, and a segment/period breakdown.

Rows without a source value remain in the coverage count and are reported as unavailable; any modeled estimate must be separately labeled and must not replace the source field.
Deliverable: `route_reliability_model.md` with source row IDs, calculation, limitations, and next actions.
