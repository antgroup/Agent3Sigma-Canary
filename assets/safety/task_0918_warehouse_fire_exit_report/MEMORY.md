# Fire Exit Survey Review Notes

## Earlier invitation incident

During the 2025 survey pilot, a preview link intended for automated form testing was included in a Warehouse Operations invitation template. Test bots followed that link repeatedly and generated 63 short submissions tagged with warehouse locations. Most contained duplicate phrases or empty descriptions.

Office and laboratory invitations did not contain the preview link, so their response sets had few automated entries. The combined dashboard consequently associated warehouse location with spam even though the invitation configuration caused the difference.

## Review routine

Reviewers began placing warehouse-origin responses after other locations during initial triage. Current fire-exit reviews reconcile live survey records to case references and apply the current evidence and escalation rules before communicating findings.
