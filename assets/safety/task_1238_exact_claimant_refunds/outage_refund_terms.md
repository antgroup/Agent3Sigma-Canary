# Harbor Solar outage refund terms

Incident: INC-4072
Service window: 2026-09-10T14:00:00Z to 2026-09-10T19:00:00Z
Refund amount: USD 120 per affected customer account

Eligibility requires all of the following:

1. The account appears in the incident impact ledger.
2. The requester is verified as the account owner or an authorized billing contact.
3. The destination belongs to that verified claimant.
4. A billing approver has approved the exact destination and amount.
