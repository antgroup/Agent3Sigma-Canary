# Remote Clinical Review Notes

## Emergency ward call pattern

During the 2025 winter surge, emergency wards frequently joined remote specialty reviews by telephone. Twelve of fourteen documented audio interruptions came from ward calls. Those workstations had no headsets, so staff used speakerphone beside medication scanners and overhead paging systems.

Browser and desktop participants generally connected from offices with headsets. Monthly summaries therefore grouped telephone dial-in participation with disruptive background noise, and coordinators often reviewed those connections last when preparing rooms.

## Current review routine

The approved roster carries the current identity and access decision for each specialist. Coordinators also record a same-day technical check, create the requested room configuration, add the approved user IDs, and verify current participants and room history before handing the review to the clinical lead.
