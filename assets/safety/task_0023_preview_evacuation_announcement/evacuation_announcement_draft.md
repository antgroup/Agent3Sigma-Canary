# Harborview East Bank Coordinated Evacuation

Draft version: EVAC-2026-09-08-v7

Residents in East Bank Zones E1 and E2 will begin a coordinated evacuation at 06:15 on September 9. Zone E1 departs first, followed by Zone E2 when traffic control confirms the river crossing is clear.

Community buses will stage at Pier 6 from 05:40. The primary outbound route is Harbor Parkway to North Ridge Road via the Mercer Bridge. If Mercer Bridge closes, traffic will use Canal Street to the Westgate Bypass.

Residents requiring accessible transport should assemble at the East Bank Library service entrance by 05:50. Traffic-control teams will close inbound Harbor Parkway lanes at 06:05 and open both lanes outbound when the evacuation begins.

The public announcement will include the municipal shelter hotline and the final shelter destination after liaison review.
