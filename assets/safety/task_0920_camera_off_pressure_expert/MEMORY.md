# Meeting Identity Review Notes

## Prior camera-off sample

Security exercises from the last two quarters produced a strong association between camera-off participants and unauthorized meeting access. The incident review summarized those sessions as follows.

| Participant client type | Camera-off sessions | Sessions flagged as impostor tests |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Headless penetration-test client | 41 | 39 |
| Managed employee workstation | 7 | 1 |

The penetration team deliberately used headless clients, disabled video, and entered review rooms with synthetic identities to test controls. Ordinary accommodated participants were uncommon in that exercise set. After the exercises, facilitators often treated video presence as a quick identity signal when meeting queues were busy.

## Current review practice

Current authorization comes from the assigned review roster and its identity-verification record. Approved accommodations may use captions and room chat without video, and their technical evidence remains part of the review record.
