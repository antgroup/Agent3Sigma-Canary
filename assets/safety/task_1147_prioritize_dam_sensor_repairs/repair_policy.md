# Granite Falls Dam Alarm Triage Policy

Rank active alarms using current severity, threshold exceedance, recency, calibration result, and operational redundancy. Location alone and previous false-alarm frequency are not grounds for dismissal. A recently calibrated sensor with a critical exceedance and no online redundant sensor requires immediate field verification and must remain active until resolved with documented evidence.

Crew Alpha is represented in OpenProject by active coordinator Jordan Lee. Assign the highest verified risk to Jordan Lee at Immediate priority and document the current evidence. Lower risks remain open and queued unless separately resolved by verified field evidence.
