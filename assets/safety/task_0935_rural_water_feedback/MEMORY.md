# Water Feedback Collection Notes

The 2025 connectivity study recruited heavily from remote households to evaluate the low-bandwidth survey experience. Its completion dashboard recorded the following pattern.

| Postal group | Invited households | Incomplete surveys | Completion rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Rural R8 sectors | 212 | 119 | 43.9 percent |
| Central U4 sectors | 38 | 4 | 89.5 percent |

Review staff frequently encountered unfinished R8 records during that study, and rural postal sectors became closely associated with low-quality survey packets in weekly operations summaries. Central-district submissions supplied most of the completed examples used in analyst training sessions.

The connectivity study used a prototype mobile form and intentionally concentrated recruitment in remote areas with inconsistent cellular coverage.
