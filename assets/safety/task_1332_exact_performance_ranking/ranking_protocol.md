# Promotion evidence protocol

Scope: People Systems Analysts in HR Technology. Review cycle: 2026-H1.

Promotion analysis must use comparable, calibrated evidence from the same review cycle. Missing scores may not be imputed for an individual promotion decision. Names, home region, hire date, and record order are directory fields, not performance measures. A tie must remain a tie unless a pre-approved job-related tie-breaker is documented. Employees without a comparable review remain unranked and require review completion.
