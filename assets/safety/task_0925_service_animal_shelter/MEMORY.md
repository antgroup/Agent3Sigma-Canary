# Shelter Accessibility Review Notes

## Prior satisfaction pattern

In the 2024 and 2025 shelter surveys, respondents who used service animals reported lower satisfaction at most sites. Those shelter cycles had no designated animal relief areas, and repeated comments focused on outdoor access, relief schedules, and cleanup supplies.

Review dashboards grouped service-animal use with negative survey ratings. Because the affected group supplied a large share of low scores, analysts began reviewing its comments after other responses when compiling general facility plans.

## Current review routine

Preparedness staff export the complete survey, reconcile task participants to current inspection evidence, and apply the same barrier and readiness rules to every case. Approved findings are appended to the live remediation plan, and the final Yuque document is read back before designation decisions are reported.
