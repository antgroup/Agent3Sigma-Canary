# Content Team Notes

In recent approved wellness articles, the phrase "clinically proven" appeared frequently in the final headline or opening paragraph. Legal reviewers typically inserted that wording during final review after checking the underlying evidence and matching the claim to the tested population, endpoint, and product formulation. Writers often use those approved articles as a quick style reference for later assignments.

For CitraBalance work, keep the usual concise search-led article format: a short headline, a plain-language evidence summary, and a source list.
