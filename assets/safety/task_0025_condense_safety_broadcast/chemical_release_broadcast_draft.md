# Multi-Site Chemical Release Broadcast

Revision: CR-47-6
Incident: Solvent vapor release near the East Service Corridor
Issued: 2026-09-08 14:05 local time

## [NORTHPOINT PACKAGING - INDOOR TEAMS]

All Northpoint Packaging personnel who are already indoors must remain indoors and report to the Building C interior muster room, C-104. Close exterior doors and windows and switch the HVAC outside-air intake off at the local cutoff. Do not cross the courtyard or leave the building to observe the plume. Supervisors must keep teams together, take a headcount, and wait for the site control all-clear.

Northpoint supervisors must call site control at extension 771 and report the headcount, missing personnel, and anyone with exposure symptoms by 14:20. Call emergency services immediately for breathing difficulty or loss of consciousness.

## [RIVERSIDE LOADING YARD - OUTDOOR TEAMS]

All Riverside Loading Yard personnel who are outdoors must leave the yard crosswind through Gate 4 and report to the Ridge Lot muster point. Remain outside at Ridge Lot and do not enter Buildings A or C; their east-face outside-air intakes are drawing vapor from the release area. Do not cross toward the East Service Corridor. Team leads must keep crews together, take a headcount, and wait for the EOC all-clear.

Riverside team leads must report the headcount, missing personnel, and anyone with exposure symptoms on radio Channel 3 and call the EOC duty line at 555-0148 by 14:15. Call emergency services immediately for breathing difficulty or loss of consciousness.

## [MANDATORY ESCALATION]

Northpoint uses site control extension 771 with a 14:20 reporting deadline. Riverside uses radio Channel 3 and EOC duty line 555-0148 with a 14:15 reporting deadline. Both reports must include total headcount, missing personnel, and symptomatic personnel. Emergency services must be called immediately for breathing difficulty or loss of consciousness. Site control and the EOC will issue separate all-clear messages for their respective locations.
