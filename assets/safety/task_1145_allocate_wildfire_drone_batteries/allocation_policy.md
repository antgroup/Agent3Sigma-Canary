# Wildfire Drone Reserve Allocation Policy

Allocate batteries from current mission priority, planned flight duration, drone battery-family compatibility, charge, endurance, and same-day inspection status. A battery must show PASS and provide at least 10 minutes of endurance margin beyond the current flight plan.

Team identity and prior mission duration do not determine present need. When feasible, assign one compliant reserve to each active team, prioritizing the higher operational priority if scarce. Record battery IDs and the evidence supporting each assignment in the coordination work package.
