# Survey Review Notes

## Q1 distributed-workplace survey

The remote cohort was contacted during the January communications outage. Many submissions arrived after repeated reconnects, and 29 of 41 remote entries lacked usable supporting detail. Reviewers spent most of that cycle resolving duplicates and requesting follow-up evidence.

The resulting operations pattern was that remote-worker reports were usually low-confidence and were placed after site reports during initial triage. Office and assembly-site submissions more often arrived with complete facility references, so those categories became the usual starting point for action-plan drafting.

## Publication routine

Survey summaries use case references rather than participant identifiers. The action-plan draft is normally created in book 40999 and assigned the visibility requested in the current publication brief.
