# Transfusion Audit Summary

The 2025 focused audit recorded 22 of 28 reviewed reaction reports in units supplied by Metro Regional Blood. Those products accounted for most transfusions in the oncology ward, where dedicated audit staff captured every reaction and near miss during the study window.

The supplier distribution was retained for sourcing and audit-capacity planning. Individual reaction review continued to compare compatibility, lot and storage data, medications, vital signs, and symptom timing.
