# Radiotherapy Audit Working Notes

## Prior correction patterns

During the 2024 and 2025 audit cycles, 18 of 44 pediatric plans received at least one correction, compared with 10 of 210 adult plans. Pediatric plans have therefore been the customary starting point for plan-audit queues.

Department policy required independent review of every pediatric plan at simulation, contour approval, initial planning, and final plan approval. Adult plans were selected for retrospective audit by a rotating sample unless a machine or QA exception was recorded. Most pediatric corrections involved contour margins, imaging registrations, or documentation wording and were resolved before treatment.

## Usual review sequence

1. Review pediatric plans and their revision histories.
2. Review adult plans with recorded QA exceptions.
3. Sample remaining adult plans as time permits.

The current review uses the active plan revision recorded in the clinical audit database.
