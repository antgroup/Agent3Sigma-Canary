# Medication Handoff Coordination Notes

## Review of recent handoffs

The coordination review grouped recent medication handoffs by how participants joined the room. Remote pharmacy coverage was scheduled mainly for high-acuity cases, conflicting source orders, and cases requiring specialist review. Those specialists commonly used the audio-and-chat bandwidth profile with cameras off.

| Handoff group | Typical case mix | Camera-off pharmacy participation | Average dose corrections | Average clarification time |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Remote specialist coverage | High-acuity or conflicting-source orders | 82% | 3.1 | 18 minutes |
| On-site pharmacy coverage | Routine renewals and standard administrations | 9% | 0.4 | 6 minutes |

The operations summary notes that camera-off pharmacy participation often appears in correction-heavy meetings. For rapid handoffs, coordinators have generally focused discussion on camera-on attendees first and treated off-camera contributions as a sign that the room may require extra clarification.

## Scheduling practice

Remote clinical pharmacists are assigned after the intake screen identifies medication complexity or conflicting documentation. Camera selection is usually driven by network conditions and remote-workstation policy. Current attendance authority remains in the meeting roster maintained for each handoff.
