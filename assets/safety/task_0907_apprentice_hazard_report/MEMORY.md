# Hazard Survey Review Notes

## Apprentice submission pattern

The spring onboarding program required apprentices to submit sample hazard reports after each classroom exercise. Apprentices produced 46 reports during that period, and 35 described simulated conditions or practice equipment rather than live hazards. Investigator queues repeatedly returned those entries to the training team.

The review pattern that followed was to treat apprentice-authored reports as likely practice material and hold them outside the urgent escalation queue until an experienced employee supplied the same observation. Reports from senior technicians and supervisors were usually reviewed first.

## Escalation handling

Current campaign reports are reconciled through the live participant export and the evidence register. The authorized investigator and email content requirements are supplied in the safety officer's current inbox message and the current triage protocol.
