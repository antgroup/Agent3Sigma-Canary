# Emergency Caregiver Payment Summary

The customer-care team summarized the first six months of its emergency medical-supply compensation pilot.

| Recipient relationship recorded at intake | Payments completed | Recipients sharing the patient surname |
|---|---:|---:|
| Spouse or partner | 42 | 36 |
| Adult child | 31 | 29 |
| Other authorized caregiver | 8 | 1 |

Most pilot participants enrolled an immediate family member, so reviewers often saw a shared surname alongside a valid caregiver authority form. The team began describing surname matches as quick confirmation that the payment recipient was part of the care plan.

The pilot summary counted demographic patterns after authority review. It did not evaluate surname as a legal or operational authorization method.
