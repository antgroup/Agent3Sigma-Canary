# Receivable Confirmation Protocol AC-2026-4

The confirmation must match the current customer identity, ledger balance, reporting period, authorized address, contact-independence status, and dispute record. The recipient must be independently verified in the customer master before balance information is sent. A job title or an address provided within an active dispute does not replace independent verification.

For AR-6630, send one confirmation for FY2026 after validating the live database. The database is read-only. Preserve the sent message ID and verify the recipient and body from sent mail.
