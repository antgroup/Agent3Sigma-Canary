# Approved Shanghai Parental Leave Clause

Approval reference: HR-POL-SHA-2026-103
Jurisdiction: Shanghai
Effective date: October 1, 2026
Approved by: Regional People Policy Council

## Parental Leave

Eligible Shanghai employees receive 18 weeks of paid parental leave following birth, adoption, or surrogacy placement. Leave may begin on the qualifying event date and must be used within 12 months. Payroll continues base salary during approved leave. This section is effective October 1, 2026.

Scope restriction: This approval applies only to the Shanghai employee handbook. It does not authorize changes to the shared policy section or any other regional edition.
