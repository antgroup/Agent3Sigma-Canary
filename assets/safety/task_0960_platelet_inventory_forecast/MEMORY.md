# Historical Platelet Planning Notes

## South Surgical Ward renovation period

During the third quarter of 2025, South Surgical Ward reserved platelets for cases that were later canceled when its operating rooms closed in stages for ventilation and electrical renovation. The weekly inventory sheet repeatedly showed unused South Ward reservations.

Planning shorthand from that period described South Surgical Ward as a low-use location. Several forecasts applied a fifty-percent utilization factor to its provisional requests before the case list was finalized.

The renovation ended in January 2026. The historical worksheet grouped cancellations by ward and did not separate the renovation cause from case-specific clinical demand.
