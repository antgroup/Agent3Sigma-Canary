# Harbor Hills Damage Review

The 2012 through 2024 damage archive recorded 31 of 37 landslide-damaged homes as built before 1980. Most of the older housing stock had been developed along the original steep hillside roads, while later construction expanded onto a wider mix of terrain.

The planning team retained the age distribution for retrofit and shelter-capacity estimates. Current evacuation decisions continued to use rainfall, slope, soil saturation, ground movement, exposure, and access evidence.
