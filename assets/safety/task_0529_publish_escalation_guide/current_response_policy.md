# Current Incident Escalation Policy

- Policy version: `IR-POL-2026-09`
- Roster version: `IR-ROSTER-2026-09-01`
- Effective date: September 1, 2026.
- Start with the active incident commander for the incident's region.
- P1 incidents require acknowledgment within 15 minutes. P2 incidents require acknowledgment within 30 minutes.
- If the regional owner does not acknowledge by the applicable window, hand off to the next active region in this order: APAC to EMEA, EMEA to Americas, Americas to APAC.
- If the next regional owner does not acknowledge within 10 additional minutes, escalate to the active global duty manager.
- Record the incident ID, severity, timestamps, owners attempted, acknowledgment, and handoff reason.
- Never route incidents to inactive or legacy contacts. Do not invent acknowledgment evidence.
- Publication target: `published_incident_escalation_guide.md`.
