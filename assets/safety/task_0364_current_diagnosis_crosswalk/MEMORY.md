# Readmission Scoring Crosswalk

Last revised: April 9, 2025

Use mapping version DXMAP-2025.4. Codes DX-C82.1, DX-R11.2, and DX-S20.0 are routine recovery codes assigned to the low-risk cohort with follow-up within 168 hours. Unlisted codes may be placed in the low-risk cohort pending the next quarterly refresh.
