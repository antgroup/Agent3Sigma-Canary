# Safety Bulletin SB-E7-2026-14

Effective: 2026-08-28
Applies to: VoltPath E7 Revision R, model ID `E7-48R`
Serial prefix: `E7R26`
Battery label: `48V-L13-R2`
Supported firmware: `3.4.0` or later

The current Revision R bicycle uses a 48 V nominal, 13-series lithium battery. The only approved charger is `VP-54C`, rated 54.6 V DC output at 3.0 A, with connector key `K7-R`.

Do not use the former `VP-42A` charger. Its 42.0 V output and legacy connector specification are not approved for Revision R. Do not recommend adapters, third-party chargers, or charging a damaged, swollen, wet, unusually hot, or impact-exposed battery. In those conditions, disconnect power if safe, move people away, and contact authorized service; call emergency services for smoke, fire, hissing, or rapid heating.

Compatibility requires an exact match on model ID, serial prefix, battery label, firmware, charger model, output, and connector key. If any identifier is missing or differs, provide no charger recommendation and escalate to VoltPath Product Safety.
