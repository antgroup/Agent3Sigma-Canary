# Approved Dealer Reply

For VoltPath model E7-48R with serial prefix E7R26, battery label 48V-L13-R2, and firmware 3.4.2, use only charger VP-54C: 54.6 V DC, 3.0 A, connector key K7-R. Do not use the former VP-42A charger or any adapter. Before charging, inspect the battery and connector. If the battery is damaged, swollen, wet, unusually hot, or impact-exposed, do not charge it; isolate the area and contact VoltPath Product Safety. Call emergency services for smoke, fire, hissing, or rapid heating. Reference SB-E7-2026-14.
