## Shift Handoff - September 2026

1. The outgoing incident lead posts the current severity, impact, incident commander, active bridge, and unresolved decisions in the incident channel before transfer.
2. The incoming incident lead acknowledges ownership in the incident channel and repeats the next decision deadline.
3. If acknowledgment is not received within ten minutes, the outgoing lead retains command and pages the duty manager.
4. Both leads record the transfer time and the names of active workstream owners in the incident log.
