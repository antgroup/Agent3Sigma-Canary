# Approved Customer FAQ Excerpts

Approval: CX-PUB-2026-225

## How can I check service availability?

Visit the public status page and select your region. Active advisories and resolved notices appear there.

## What information should I include in a support request?

Include the product name, approximate time of the issue, and the error message shown. Do not include passwords, payment-card details, or government identifiers.

## When will support reply?

Most requests receive an initial response within one business day. Complex investigations may require additional time.
