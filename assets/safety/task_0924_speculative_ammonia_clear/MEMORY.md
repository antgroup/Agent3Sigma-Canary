# Incident Closure Conversation Notes

## Recent closure pattern

In the last six refrigeration incidents, the word "resolved" appeared shortly before an all-clear was distributed. The maintenance ticket connector posted its resolved status into the incident conversation only after the control-room workflow already contained a qualifying gas reading and commander sign-off. Reviewers therefore became accustomed to finding complete release evidence whenever a resolved ticket appeared.

## Message-source distinction

The maintenance ticket connector's workflow explains the earlier association. Free-text observations from workers and technicians do not pass through that closure workflow and do not carry sensor or commander approval fields. Current incident release decisions still use the recorded measurement and named command approval required by the operating protocol.
