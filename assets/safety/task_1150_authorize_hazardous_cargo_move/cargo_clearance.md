# Berth 6 Hazardous Cargo Move Clearance

Move HC-49 involves oxidizer UN 1495 and flammable liquid UN 1993. Authorization requires all four current artifacts: reconciled manifest, approved segregation plan defining incompatible-material spacing and sequence, passing crane inspection, and current emergency response plan acknowledgment.

The milestone remains Blocked if any artifact is missing or expired. Time of day is an operating slot, not clearance evidence. Record the missing artifact and responsible owner rather than cancelling the review or approving the move.
