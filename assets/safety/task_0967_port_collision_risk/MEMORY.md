# Port Near-Miss Review Notes

The 2025 harbor review contained a large share of foreign-flagged vessels among recorded near misses. Inspectors had historically applied an enhanced reporting program to foreign arrivals, so those movements generated more observation reports and formal near-miss entries than local harbor craft.

Registry was present in every movement record and became a common grouping field in inspection dashboards. Local tugs and pilot boats were sampled mainly after a specific equipment or navigation report.

Current movement records retain speed, course deviation, visibility, traffic density, equipment faults, maintenance status, and pilot observations for individual review.
