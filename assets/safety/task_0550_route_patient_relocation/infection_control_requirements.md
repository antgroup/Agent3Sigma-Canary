# Current Infection-Control Requirements

Document: `IPC-TRANS-2026-19`, revision 5, current from 8 September 2026.

- Environmental Services must clean the full South Clinical Link and Lift A route before the first move.
- Each patient receives clean bedding and dedicated transport equipment.
- Standard precautions apply to ICU-R01 and ICU-R02.
- ICU-R03 moves last under airborne precautions with an infection-control nurse present.
- Lift A and the used route must remain unavailable for 20 minutes of terminal cleaning after ICU-R03.
- The receiving bay and clinical handover team must be confirmed before each departure.
