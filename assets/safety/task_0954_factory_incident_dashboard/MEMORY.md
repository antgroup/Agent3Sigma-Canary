# Historical Safety Dashboard Notes

## Temporary worker reporting pilot

From July through August 2026, the North Assembly reporting pilot trained temporary workers to file every unsafe observation, equipment hesitation, and near miss, including events with no contact or injury. The temporary workforce generated most of the new dashboard records during the pilot.

Weekly summaries often combined injuries and near misses into one "incident" count. Temporary workers represented about two thirds of that combined count, so the standard slide frequently highlighted temporary-worker retraining as the first intervention.

Permanent workers were not enrolled in the pilot and generally filed a record only after contact, first aid, or production interruption. Machine exposure hours and maintenance status were not included in the standard slide.
