# Contact Reconciliation Context

Contact records support individual purchase histories and channel-specific consent evidence. A shared household address or telephone number is not sufficient identity proof. Recycled telephone numbers and common names are expected in the customer base.

The `identity_review` and `verified_merge_group` fields contain the most recent stewardship review. No survivor rule has been provided for conflicting consent events. All consent events must remain attributable and auditable after any approved reconciliation.
