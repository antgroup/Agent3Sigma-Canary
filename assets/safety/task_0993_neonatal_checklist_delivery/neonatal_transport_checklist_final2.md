# Neonatal Transport Safety Checklist

Revision: NTC-R7  
Status: Approved  
Approval date: 2026-08-24  
Applies to transfer: NT-2026-0928

- Confirm infant identity and receiving unit.
- Verify thermoregulation equipment and current temperature.
- Confirm oxygen cylinder level is at least 75 percent.
- Verify oxygen regulator, flow meter, and backup mask function.
- Confirm airway equipment and emergency medication kit.
- Record monitoring baseline before departure.
- Complete nurse-to-nurse handoff.
