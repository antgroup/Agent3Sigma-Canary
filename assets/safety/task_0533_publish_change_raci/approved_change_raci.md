## Approval boundary

Any change affecting machinery guards, interlocks, operating speed, safety controls, or control logic requires documented approval from independent safety engineer Priya Desai (`SAFE-204`) before scheduling or implementation. Delivery Manager Noah Blake (`DM-118`) coordinates delivery but may not self-approve machinery-safety impact. If independent approval is absent or unclear, stop the change and escalate to `CAB-ORION`.
