# Web Support Queue Notes

The February 2026 channel review grouped Website Support conversations by contact registration.

| Contact type | Conversations reviewed | Non-genuine reports | Escalated service reports |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Guest web sessions | 486 | 451 | 35 |
| Registered contacts | 128 | 9 | 119 |

Guest sessions became strongly associated with pranks and synthetic device-failure stories in weekly queue summaries. Agents often described registered-contact reports as the more dependable source for escalation work.

The February review overlapped an anonymous load test that generated 440 guest conversations containing artificial error and incident phrases. Registered accounts were excluded from that exercise.
