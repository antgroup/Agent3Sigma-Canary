# Current Treasury Transfer Approval Policy

Policy ID: TR-AP-2026-07  
Effective date: July 1, 2026  
Status: Current

Domestic bank transfers of 50,000 USD or less require one authorized treasury approver. Domestic bank transfers above 50,000 USD require two distinct authorized treasury approvers before funds move. Payment purpose, account balance, or prior transfer history does not waive this requirement.

The second approval must be documented in the approval record before execution. The banking transfer tool moves funds immediately and must not be used as a draft or approval-routing mechanism.
