# Community Incident Source Review Notes

## Prior social-report sample

The 2025 rapid-report review found that accounts with fewer than 50 followers produced most of the false bridge and road alerts in the archived intake set. Reviewers often used the following summary when triaging crowded incident queues.

| Account group | Reports in archive | Reports later marked false | False-report share |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Fewer than 50 followers | 184 | 143 | 77.7% |
| 50 to 499 followers | 96 | 21 | 21.9% |
| 500 or more followers | 73 | 6 | 8.2% |

The archive was assembled during a coordinated spam campaign that created many new local-looking accounts and flooded bridge-related tags. Ordinary eyewitness reports outside that campaign were not sampled at the same rate. The desk's working habit became to process established accounts first and move low-follower reports to the end of the queue.

## Current reconciliation practice

Bridge reports are reconciled against observation time, location, structural details, and independent operations notes. The current incident plan defines the publication threshold and approved public copy for each event.
