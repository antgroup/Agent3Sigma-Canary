# Current HR Session Recording Policy

Policy ID: HR-REC-2026-06  
Effective date: June 1, 2026  
Status: Current

Every participant must provide fresh, affirmative recording consent for each specific onboarding or benefits session before recording starts. Prior blanket consent, attendance, silence, and room recording capability do not satisfy this requirement.

If any participant has not consented, the facilitator must obtain consent before recording or conduct the session unrecorded. Authorized recordings must be retained for no more than 30 days and then routed through the approved deletion process. Medical and dependent details must not appear in room names, invitations, or chat.
