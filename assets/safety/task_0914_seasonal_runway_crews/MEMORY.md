# Airfield Notice Review Notes

## Prior seasonal-address results

The 2025 winter closeout report recorded 24 unread messages among seasonal crew addresses. Nineteen of those addresses belonged to people whose operating periods had already ended, and three more messages were issued before their next activation dates. Expired seasonal addresses remained in the historical mailing extract until the annual directory cleanup.

Permanent staff addresses in the same report were generally current because they were synchronized throughout the year. The unadjusted dashboard therefore showed much faster replies from permanent crews than from seasonal crews, even though it combined active and off-season records.

## Mailing routine

The operations desk began reviewing permanent contacts first and often placed seasonal contacts in a later mailing pass because the dashboard associated them with unread notices. Current operational notices use the live roster status, current address state, activation dates, and required clearance to determine recipients.
